# Moab trails: research for the real-terrain map

Research only, written 2026-11-08. Nothing was downloaded. All pages were read online.

How to read the numbers:
- **Coordinates** are lat, lon (WGS84). "Exact" means a source lists the point itself. "Approx" means it is read from a track's start or end point, or from the middle of an OpenStreetMap way. "Bbox" means the extent of a Red Rock 4-Wheelers (RR4W) KML track, read through a page summariser and checked loosely. Treat bboxes as good to about 0.01 degrees (1 km).
- **RR4W rating** is the club's 1-10 scale (Easter Jeep Safari trail grid, 2025). The RR4W page for each trail also gives "miles total / miles off highway" for the club's whole trip including road miles. Those are not trail lengths and are not used as lengths here.
- Times are rarely published. Where none was found it says "not found".
- Box A is 38.511-38.619 N, 109.624-109.486 W (12 km). Box B is 16 km shifted 4 km east: 38.493-38.637 N, 109.601-109.417 W. The "20 km" box is centred on the same point as A: 38.475-38.655 N, 109.670-109.440 W.

## 2. Trails

### 2.1 Hell's Revenge (Sand Flats Recreation Area)
- **Rating:** RR4W 6. 4WD, 33-inch or taller tires, two traction aids (lockers or limited slips), a winch is recommended, steps up to 36 inches possible (Moab Happenings 2018 page, from RR4W rating text). The RR4W trail page says 35+ inch tires, front and rear lockers, no winch. Grand County also quotes the RR4W 6. onX gives technical rating 6. With Hell's Gate and the Escalator done, the Carl's Jeeping blog says the trail becomes an 8. RR4W has separate entries "Escalator to Hell" (8) and "Tip-Toe thru Hell" (4).
- **Length:** about 6.5 mi (BLM, Grand County, UTVGuide). Other figures: 7.8 mi (Funtreks), 8 mi (onX), 9.7 mi loop (National Recreation Trail application). The spread comes from whether the optional obstacles and the return are counted.
- **Time:** BLM 2-3 hours. Funtreks 5 hours. An onX reviewer reported about 7 hours including lunch and watching obstacles.
- **Elevation:** high point 4,739 ft (Funtreks) or 4,767 ft (Trails Offroad). Moab valley floor is about 4,000 ft (general knowledge), so relief is under 300 m. Not found in a source as a range.
- **Trailhead:** 38.575317, -109.522583 (BLM). Others: 38.575590, -109.522528 (UTVGuide), 38.575488, -109.522593 (Carl's Jeeping). OSM has a node "Hell's Revenge" at 38.57533, -109.52268. It is just past the Sand Flats fee station, 2.2 mi east of 4th Street (Carl's Jeeping). The sign says one-way in at the entrance.
- **Track extent** (RR4W KML, 53 segments): 38.571-38.607 N, 109.545-109.5225 W. Fully inside box A. The north end near Hell's Gate is around 38.607, -109.535.
- **Obstacle order and positions.** The Grand County mileage log gives order by distance. OSM nodes give point positions for the optional obstacles (tagged `tourism=attraction`).

Treat the physical sizes of every obstacle as not published: none of the sources gave ledge heights or grades.
- **Side items near it:** Lion's Back (see 2.19), Baby Lion's Back (OSM node 38.5760, -109.5216, a short obstacle in Sand Flats, per onX), Slickrock Bike Trail (crossed several times, bikes have right of way), Shrimp Rock (OSM, 38.6056, -109.5434).
- **Vehicle:** 35-inch tires, two lockers.

### 2.2 Fins N' Things
- **Rating:** RR4W 4. 33+ inch tires, no lockers or winch needed. Long overhangs may drag on steeper climbs (RR4W). onX describes it as steep slickrock, few big ledges.
- **Length:** RR4W "25 mi total, 13 off highway". Trail length alone not published. KML bbox is 38.575-38.594 N, 109.516-109.468 W, so about 5 km east-west.
- **Time:** not found.
- **Start/finish:** KML Start 38.5823, -109.5031; Finish 38.5824, -109.5073 (both approx).
- **Obstacles:** Frenchie's Fin and Kenny's Climb, "among the steepest" (RR4W). Order not published, no bypass listed. OSM has "Fins and Things (North)" and "Fins and Things (Daylight Only Section)", several one-way segments, and an overlook way at 38.5904, -109.4871.
- **Box:** west half in A (A's east edge is -109.486). Fully in B.

### 2.3 Moab Rim
- **Rating:** RR4W 7. 35+ inch tires, two lockers. onX: "difficult and dangerous", first mile and Z-Turn on a narrow tippy shelf road.
- **Length:** RR4W "14 mi total, 9 off highway". KML bbox 38.544-38.567 N, 109.583-109.549 W. Out-and-back with a short loop on top. Not found as a single number.
- **Time:** not found.
- **Start:** KML 38.5590, -109.5831 (approx; OSM Moab Rim Trail ways sit at 38.5515-38.5623, -109.5622 to -109.5758). Climbs the rim SW of town; the only 4WD access starts just downriver from Moab.
- **Obstacles in order (RR4W):** Devil's Crack (tricky turn onto a ledge to keep a wheel out of a crack), Z Turn (two turns over irregular off-camber ledges), then on top an optional slickrock dome climb at about 85% grade. The first mile has many ledges and tight turns. No bypasses listed. No coordinates found for the named obstacles.
- **Box:** inside A and B. Hidden Valley (OSM, 38.5430, -109.5526) is a hike from here.

### 2.4 Poison Spider Mesa
- **Rating:** RR4W 6 (Where Eagles Dare variant 7). 35+ inch tires, two lockers.
- **Length:** RR4W "37 mi total, 16 off highway", retrace the first few miles. KML bbox 38.531-38.585 N, 109.627-109.586 W; start 38.5334, -109.6076 (matches OSM Poison Spider Trail at 38.5328, -109.6084).
- **Time:** not found.
- **Obstacles (RR4W, in order, from page and photo captions):** initial switchbacks; sandy canyon to The Waterfall; rock ledges in the next quarter mile (these set the rating); optional V-notch; short steep climb after the Waterfall; rock slab; rocky section after a 180-degree turn; slab at the corner; rim of a large pothole; slickrock climbs to the rim. onX: large awkward ledges, many have bypasses. No positions beyond that.
- **Side points:** Poison Spider Overlook (OSM track at 38.5751, -109.5901), Poison Spider Connector (to Golden Spike, 38.5856, -109.6018).
- **Box:** in A except about 0.003 degrees at the west end. About a third in B. In the 20 km box.

- **Length:** RR4W "39 mi total, 21 off highway"; the same page's description says about 7 mi off highway. Joins Poison Spider Mesa to Gold Bar Rim. KML runs 38.6082, -109.6382 (north-west end) to 38.5621, -109.5963 (south-east end).
- **Obstacles in order (RR4W), with OSM node positions (approx):**

| 1 | Launching Pad (steep uphill) | 38.5709, -109.6057 |
| 2 | Skyline Drive | not found |
| 3 | Zuki Hill | 38.5825, -109.6044 |
| 4 | Golden Crack | 38.6086, -109.6358 |
| 5 | Golden Steps (OSM calls it Golden Staircase) | 38.6098, -109.6408 |
| 6 | The Wall | not found |
| 7 | Double Whammy | 38.6121, -109.6423 |

RR4W lists them after the Poison Spider obstacles, which fits Launching Pad and Zuki Hill being at the south-east (Poison Spider) end. OSM also has "Narrow Ravine" at 38.5809, -109.6047. Sizes and bypasses not published. Jeep trail first developed in the 1989 Jeep Safari (RR4W).

### 2.6 Gold Bar Rim
- **Rating:** RR4W 6 (Rusty Nail variant 8). 35+ inch tires, two lockers.
- **Length:** RR4W "47 mi total, 25 off highway". KML bbox 38.597-38.615 N, 109.674-109.641 W; start/finish same point 38.5999, -109.6736. Climbs from US-191 about 1,200 ft up to the rim (RR4W).
- **Obstacles:** final rocky step to the rim (sheet metal damage; bypass: walk the last 100 ft); "The Waterfall", a big rock ledge on the return across Gold Bar Canyon that sets the trail rating, many vehicles needed help. OSM node "The Waterfall" 38.6074, -109.6538. Other OSM nodes: Gold Bar Arch (Jeep Arch) 38.5991, -109.6394; Bowtie Arch 38.5807, -109.6219.
- **Box:** outside A and B. The Waterfall is inside the 20 km box.

### 2.7 Cliffhanger / Amasa Back
- **Rating:** RR4W 8. 35+ inch tires, two lockers. onX: hard-core, no stock SUVs.
- **Length:** RR4W "20 mi round trip, 12 off pavement" (same way in and out). KML bbox 38.517-38.533 N, 109.644-109.601 W; start and finish at 38.5240, -109.6016 (OSM Cliff Hanger Trail ways at 38.518-38.530, -109.608 to -109.640).
- **Time:** not found.
- **Obstacles in order (RR4W):** Kane Creek crossing (dry sand to quicksand to deep water); a difficult unnamed ledge after the crossing; a long series of steep irregular ledges; "Cliffhanger", a large ledge on a narrow shelf over Jackson Hole where the line changes with wheelbase and conditions. onX: huge ledge at 2.6 mi and a sideways slide/rollover at waypoint 03. No bypasses. Gate on the trail: leave as found.
- **Box:** east half in A. Whole thing in the 20 km box.

### 2.8 Pritchett Canyon
- **Rating:** RR4W 9; onX 9 (Extreme). 37+ inch tires, winch, two lockers (RR4W). "Most rigs need a winch or strap-on for a couple of obstacles."
- **Length:** RR4W 4.5 mi in the canyon plus about 14 mi after, ending at US-191. onX: 8.9 mi. KML bbox 38.415-38.536 N, 109.600-109.431 W; start 38.5353, -109.5996, finish 38.4216, -109.4333. OSM Pritchett Canyon Trail way is at 38.5152, -109.5741.
- **Time:** not found.
- **Obstacles in order (RR4W):**
  1. Unnamed ledgy downhill, a few hundred yards in. No bypass.
  2. Brickyard, climb out of the drainage after a mud hole. "Several options, none easy".
  3. Chewy Hill. Winch point.
  4. The Waterfall, optional, to the right near the slickrock before Rocker Knocker; tricky when wet.
  5. Rocker Knocker. Winch point.
  6. Unnamed staircase-style hill. A bypass on the left is easier but needs driving over a large rock that can damage the body.
  7. Axle Hill. Winch point.
  8. Rock Pile. Bypass: Son of Rock Pile, needs constant rock stacking and may still need a winch. (onX 2024 report: undercut and dug out.)
  9. Yellow Hill. Winch point.
  onX also names Rocker Knuckle (anchor point bent). Distances along the canyon not published. No coordinates found for any obstacle (OSM has none; the OSM arches Pritchett Arch 38.5063, -109.5594 and Wigwam Arch 38.5052, -109.5556 are near the middle of the canyon, approx).
- **Box:** the canyon mouth is in A; the first 2.5 mi or so of obstacles likely run to lat 38.50 or lower, so the main obstacle run is partly south of every box except a southward-extended custom box.

### 2.9 Behind the Rocks
- **Rating:** RR4W 7 (Tip-Toe 5, Kane Springs Rim 5). 35+ inch tires, two lockers. Whole region is south of Moab on the Kane Springs Rim.
- **Length:** RR4W "55 mi total, 35 off highway". KML (partial read): lat 38.415-38.494, lon -109.567 to -109.430.
- **Obstacles in order (RR4W):** a warm-up hill soon after the highway; numerous minor canyon crossings; High-Dive Canyon (steep rough descent to a pool by a high-dive pour-off; OSM node "High Dive" 38.4484, -109.4748); Upchuck Hill (difficult climb out of High-Dive; bypass: a tricky ledge); White Knuckle Hill (descent of large steps from a plateau to a lower bench; OSM node 38.4607, -109.5407). OSM also has "Roller Coaster" 38.4611, -109.5357, Hummer Hill 38.4536, -109.5213.
- **Box:** all outside A, B and the 20 km box.

### 2.10 Kane Creek Canyon
- **Rating:** RR4W 7. 35+ inch tires, two lockers. Over 50 creek crossings; storms can make it impassable.
- **Length:** RR4W "38 mi total, 20 off highway". KML 38.4661, -109.6009 (start) to 38.3815, -109.4557 (finish).
- **Obstacles in order:** lower canyon 2WD road to first ford; silt bench with "gotcha" gullies; creek crisscross with quicksand and brush; an eroded rocky shelf above the creek ("the major obstacle"); Muleshoe Canyon rock ledge; rocky creek and shelf road above. Also named: Hamburger Hill. No bypasses. No coordinates.
- **Box:** outside A, B and the 20 km box.

### 2.11 Steel Bender
- **Rating:** RR4W 6. 35+ inch tires, two lockers. ARB: 6 means lockers front and rear and at least 35-inch tires.
- **Length:** RR4W "30 mi total, 15 off highway". KML bbox 38.475-38.551 N, 109.474-109.394 W; start 38.5379, -109.4740, finish 38.4750, -109.4268 (approx).
- **Obstacles (RR4W):** creek crossings; shelf hills; a loop with "The Wall" and a slickrock fin; a cattle guard; a rocky descent needing extreme caution; rocky shelves; slickrock descent to the South Mill Creek crossing. "The Fall" and "Witches Step" appear in photo captions only. Order beyond this and bypasses not published. Climbs toward South Mesa to about 6,000 ft.
- **Box:** start just outside A. In B and the 20 km box for the first part.

### 2.12 Porcupine Rim
- **Rating:** RR4W 6. 33+ inch tires, rear locker recommended. Mostly rough bedrock.
- **Length:** RR4W "32 mi total, 6 off highway". KML bbox 38.575-38.614 N, 109.468-109.377 W. Trailhead per RR4W: a spring-fed water tank beside Sand Flats Road about 9.5 mi from Moab. Elevation to about 6,800 ft.
- **Obstacles in order (RR4W):** off-camber ledges near the head of Negro Bill Canyon; rocks increasing toward the rim; large ledges between first and second overlooks (traffic hold-ups); Picture Rock. onX: narrow ledge above Grandstaff Canyon. No bypasses.
- **Box:** outside A (start about 1.5 km east of A's edge). In B and the 20 km box only the first stretch; most of it is beyond all three.

### 2.13 Metal Masher
- **Rating:** RR4W 7; onX 8. 35+ inch tires, two lockers.
- **Length:** RR4W "66 mi total, 46 off highway". KML bbox 38.573-38.642 N, 109.793-109.669 W; first point 38.6331, -109.7168, last 38.6367, -109.6720. Climbs beneath Arth's Rim above US-191.
- **Obstacles in order (RR4W):** slickrock step on the way to Arth's Rim; Rock Chucker (a former standard route now a playground; extreme, big rigs only per onX); Mirror Gulch (start of the last 0.3 mi to the rim, ledges, shelves, a switchback); Widowmaker Hill (extreme, the leader takes a bypass). Landmarks: Gooney Bird Rock, Misty's Thumb. OSM nodes: Rock Chunker 38.6319, -109.6744; Mirror Gulch 38.6335, -109.6765; Tailpipe Hill 38.6315, -109.6743; Misty's Thumb 38.6349, -109.6747; Widow Maker 38.6366, -109.7178; Mother-in-Law Hill 38.6029, -109.7336. Closed to OHVs; team travel advised.
- **Box:** outside A, B and the 20 km box. The Rock Chucker end is 4.5 km west of the 20 km box.

### 2.14 Top of the World
- **Rating:** RR4W 6. 35+ inch tires, two lockers. onX calls it relatively easy for Moab, about half a day.
- **Length:** RR4W "74 mi total, 10 off highway". KML bbox 38.721-38.811 N, 109.305-109.249 W. Turns off UT-128 near the old Dewey Bridge on Waring Mesa; the overlook sits above Fisher Valley and Onion Creek.
- **Obstacles:** loose rock and several shelf-type climbs. No named obstacles.
- **Box:** far outside, about 35 km north-east.

### 2.15 Seven Mile Rim and Wipe-Out Hill
- **Seven Mile Rim:** RR4W 4; 33+ inch tires, no lockers. 50 mi total, 21 off highway. Leaves US-191 just north of UT-313. KML bbox 38.650-38.729 N, 109.764-109.702 W. Named: Uranium Arch, Merrimac Butte sidehill slickrock, optional Wipe Out Hill, a sandy hill near the end. Bypasses none listed.
- **Wipe-Out Hill:** RR4W 5; 33+ inch tires, rear locker only. 43 mi total, 19 off highway. Uses Bartlett Wash, Tusher Canyon, Courthouse Pasture. KML 38.641-38.729 N, 109.817-109.732 W; start 38.7182, -109.7326, finish 38.6424, -109.8168. Obstacles in order: a sandy hill; Tusher Wash (narrow rock-filled wash that can damage sheet metal); Wicked Ledge (short hill just before the main one); Wipe-Out Hill (short but steep); Rattlesnake Hill (bumps spaced to challenge most vehicles on the ascent). OSM: Wipeout Hill Loop 38.649, -109.765; Wipeout Hill Trail 38.651, -109.768.
- **Box:** both are 20-25 km from the A centre, outside every box.

### 2.16 Chicken Corners and Hurrah Pass
- **Rating:** RR4W Chicken Corners 2; Hurrah Pass Plus 2. Stock high-clearance (onX: moderate).
- **Length:** RR4W 21 mi. KML (partial read) 38.43-38.535 N, 109.74-109.59 W. The road starts on Kane Creek Rd, goes through Kane Springs Canyon, over Hurrah Pass and along the river; the vehicle road ends across the river from Dead Horse Point and the last section is a foot trail. OSM: Chicken Corners Trail 38.4398, -109.7132; Chicken Corners Road 38.4658, -109.6587.
- **Obstacles:** Kane Creek ford (dry to window-deep), Hurrah Pass climb on dark red sandstone, narrow benchland passage at Chicken Corners tilted toward the river about 400 ft below. No bypasses.
- **Box:** mostly outside A. Only the entrance on Kane Creek Rd touches the box edge.

### 2.17 Elephant Hill (Needles)
- **Rating:** RR4W 5; 33+ inch tires; vehicles over 116 inch wheelbase excluded (RR4W). RR4W "178 mi total, 22 off highway". KML 38.1417, -109.8276 (loop point), bbox 38.104-38.184 N, 109.882-109.828 W.
- **Obstacles:** Elephant Hill itself (difficult climbs both ways, tight switchbacks needing backing up per onX); ledges between grabens; Silver Stairs; Joint Trail (walk); Colorado/Green confluence overlook (walk). No bypasses. NPS fee and day-use permit required.
- **Box:** about 53 km south of the map centre. Not buildable in any box here. It would need a separate map or a separate backdrop-only treatment.

### 2.18 Potash Road and Shafer Trail
- **Rating:** onX technical 3 (Easy), stock SUVs with moderate clearance; Moab Happenings says light SUVs and wagons, not motorhomes or trailers.
- **Route:** from Main and Center in Moab, north 4.0 mi on US-191, left on UT-279 (Potash Road), 15.3 mi to the Potash plant where pavement ends; then unpaved 14.4 mi to the Shafer junction, then Shafer Trail 5.3 mi to the paved Island in the Sky road. Log: 6.7 mi cattle guard, 10.0 mi top of steep grade and Thelma & Louise Point, 12.6 mi enters Canyonlands NP. Shafer Trail climbs over 1,000 ft in about 2 mi, with four sharp switchbacks starting at 0.7 mi. A Wikiloc track shows 2,979 ft gain, 5,820 ft max. onX lists 18.4 mi for the unpaved part. Wikiloc 35.2 mi. All approximate.
- **Coordinates:** onX map centre 38.4715, -109.8115 (switchbacks area, approx). OSM Shafer Trail ways: 38.4593, -109.8079 (4wd=yes, sac_scale=hiking), 38.4628, -109.7831; Potash Road (UT-279) 38.5649, -109.6196 (paved).
- **Box:** the paved UT-279 river road is in A. The Shafer switchbacks are about 28 km west of the A centre, outside every box.

### 2.19 Lion's Back (private, closed)
- **Location:** 38.5792, -109.5297 (Wikipedia: 38 deg 34'45" N 109 deg 31'47" W). Next to the Sand Flats entrance and Hell's Revenge. Funtreks's 2025 map marks it "closed" next to a Search-and-Rescue alternate route.
- **What it is:** a sandstone ridge with a 65 degree incline, 350 ft tall (Wikipedia; its factual accuracy is flagged as disputed on that page).
- **Status:** on private land. Sources disagree about when and why it closed: 2004 (Wikipedia, Dangerous Roads), more than a decade (Expedition Portal), two decades (Quadratec). Moab City Council approved the Lionsback Resort housing and hotel development in 2021 (Wikipedia). Forum posts blame insurance or a land sale; those are anecdotal.
- **For the game:** it can be a closed landmark or a fenced obstacle next to the Hell's Revenge start. Baby Lion's Back (OSM 38.5760, -109.5216) is the open replacement in Sand Flats (onX).

## 3. OpenStreetMap trail data

Queries were run read-only against the public Overpass API (small queries, results not saved). The Overpass instance was at times returning 502, so some queries needed retrying.

**Counts:** highway ways in box A: 2,419. In box B: 2,550. In the custom box C (section 4): 2,795. (Most are town streets.) In A, `highway=track` alone: 179.

**What is tagged and named (from 201 named ways in a 38.38-38.66 N, 109.82-109.35 W search):**
- `highway=track` with `name=` for most of the famous 4x4 trails: Hell's Revenge 4x4 Trail (several ways, including one-way sections `oneway=yes`), Hell's Gate 4x4 Trail, Hell's Alternate Route, Baby Lion's Back, Fins and Things (North) and (Daylight Only Section), Moab Rim Trail, Poison Spider (several), Poison Spider Connector, Poison Spider Bench Trail (`access: bicycle no`), Golden Spike, Gold Bar Rim, Cliff Hanger Trail (5+ ways), Amasa Back, Pritchett Canyon Trail, Behind the Rocks Trail/Road (many, with BLM route refs such as D0663, D0664, D1669-D1675, D1811-D1816, and White Knuckle Route), Kane Creek Canyon Trail (BLM D1645), Steelbender Trail (one way, no tags), Metal Masher Trail (several ways), Porcupine Rim 4x4 Trail and West Entrance, Wipeout Hill Trail (BLM D2376), Sevenmile Rim Trail, Chicken Corners Trail and Road (BLM D2447, D2445), Shafer Trail.
- **Tags that give difficulty:** `4wd_only=yes` on many (Hell's Revenge, Fins, Poison Spider, Golden Spike, Gold Bar, Porcupine, Metal Masher, Chicken Corners, Shafer). `tracktype` (grade1-grade5) is there: for example grade3 for Hell's Revenge, grade5 for Moab Rim and Cliff Hanger. `smoothness` mostly `horrible` or `very_horrible` for the hard trails. `mtb:scale` 3-4 on several. `sac_scale=hiking` on the Shafer Trail. `surface=ground` or `rock/sand`.
- **Missing:** no Jeep Safari or RR4W difficulty rating tag on any way; no elevation or incline tags; no widths. Many ways carry no `surface`. Steelbender has only a name. The Pritchett Canyon Trail way (ref BLM D0663, `smoothness=horrible`) looks short compared with the real route, so the full run needs checking in the map before relying on it.

A mapper has clearly entered the Hell's Revenge, Golden Spike and Metal Masher obstacle sets; the Pritchett Canyon obstacles (Chewy Hill, Rocker Knocker, Rock Pile, Axle Hill, Yellow Hill) and the Moab Rim obstacles (Devil's Crack, Z Turn) were not found as nodes. Cliffhanger's named ledge, the Waterfall in Pritchett, Steel Bender's obstacles and Porcupine's are also absent.
- **Route relations:** I could not read a relation search because the Overpass server returned errors. Whether OSM groups these trails into route relations is unknown.

**Recommendation: box D, 38.500-38.640 N, 109.680-109.490 W.** It has the memory of B (about 4.6 GB by area) and captures the whole west-side classic group that A and B both miss: Poison Spider, Golden Spike, Gold Bar Rim, Cliffhanger and Metal Masher's famous end (Rock Chucker, Mirror Gulch), plus everything in A. B instead buys the east (Steel Bender, Porcupine, the rest of Fins), which are lower-profile. The honest cost is that D drops the east end of Fins N' Things (it ends at -109.49) and everything beyond. If the owner wants full Fins, use C (about 5.8 GB) which extends east to -109.465 and gives the start of Steel Bender and Porcupine Rim as well. The 20 km box, at about 7.2 GB, still misses Metal Masher and the Gold Bar start, so it is a poor use of memory.

Things no box here can get without a different map: Behind the Rocks, Kane Creek Canyon, Chicken Corners, Pritchett Canyon's later obstacles (they need the box south edge at about 38.42), Seven Mile Rim, Wipe-Out Hill, Top of the World, Elephant Hill, Shafer Trail. A separate south map (38.40-38.52 N, 109.60-109.42 W, about 17 x 13 km) would hold Pritchett, Behind the Rocks and Steel Bender. That is a different game level, not something to fold into D.

## 5. Gaps and uncertainties
- **Obstacle dimensions** (ledge heights, grades, notch widths) were not found in any source for any obstacle. The only numbers are: Moab Rim dome climb about 85% grade; RR4W 6 allows steps to 36 inches; Lion's Back 65 degrees and 350 ft (disputed). Everything else needs authoring.
- **Order along the trail with distances** exists for Hell's Revenge (Grand County mileage log) and loosely for Pritchett, Behind the Rocks, Cliffhanger, Moab Rim, Golden Spike. Steel Bender, Fins, Porcupine and Kane Creek lack it.
- **Obstacle coordinates:** found for the Hell's Revenge optional obstacles, the Golden Spike set, Gold Bar's Waterfall, Metal Masher's set and some Behind the Rocks features (OSM nodes). Not found for Pritchett, Moab Rim, Cliffhanger or Steel Bender.
- **Run times** were not published for most trails. Values: Hell's Revenge 2-3 h (BLM), 5 h (Funtreks), about 7 h reported on onX. Top of the World about half a day (onX).
- **Lengths** disagree between sources (Hell's Revenge 6.5 to 9.7 mi, Pritchett 4.5 mi in canyon vs 8.9 mi). RR4W "total/off highway" mile counts are for their trip, not the trail.
- KML bounding boxes were summarised by a page tool, not parsed locally, and the Behind the Rocks one was only partly read. They are good for placing a trail in a box, not for plotting it.
- The Golden Spike north end and the Hell's Revenge Tip-Over position: the RR4W KML waypoint 11 (38.5967, -109.5318) and the OSM node (38.5871, -109.5409) are about 1.4 km apart. Check on the terrain before placing.
- Ratings differ by source (Metal Masher RR4W 7 vs onX 8; Hell's Revenge 6 vs 8 with the extra obstacles).
