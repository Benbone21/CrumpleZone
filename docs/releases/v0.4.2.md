# Crumple Zone v0.4.2

**Multiplayer protocol 8: everyone must update to 0.4.2 to play together (0.4.0 can't join).**

## Multiplayer
- Join codes: ESC > MULTIPLAYER > OPEN TO FRIENDS shows a 6-letter code; friends use JOIN WITH CODE. No port forwarding (direct when possible, relayed otherwise). New multiplayer menus.
- Crashes now happen at the same moment and place on both machines (trucks are shown where they really are, not where they were).
- Slow computers no longer show their truck in slow motion to others.
- Field repairs, the hood, jumper cables and the horn are seen and heard by everyone.

## World
- Backcountry is now 6 x 6 km: eight districts, Elk River with fords and pools, Bear Falls and Upper Falls.
- 100 places to find: a ranger station, fuel stops, cabins, lookouts, mines (the lost mine), crossings, summits, photo spots, 60 caches.
- Rock hitboxes follow the rock's real shape. Faster loading; worlds are cached.

## Driving and repair
- 10% more tire grip; street/AT/mud tires warm up.
- Parking brake on P (pause moved to F6), horn (7), crouch (Left Ctrl).
- Open the hood and fix a flooded engine; batteries can go flat; jump pack and jumper cables.
- Every repair is REMOVE then FIT; repair and tool sounds.
- New 3D garage shop (G).

## People
- New character models; first-person body no longer sits or drags when you look down.
