# Crumple Zone v0.4.8

- **Procedural world, a real landscape:** rivers drain downhill from the high ground and join as they go, and big rivers are crossed at known fords or on worn timber bridges with missing planks. Lakes fill valleys behind natural dams, and waterfalls sit where the land really drops. Summits reach 1.5–2.5 km. The land is worn by real erosion. A thicker alpine forest has undergrowth, fallen logs and stumps, and trees reach to the horizon.
- **Career:** NEW WORLD splits the maps into BUILT MAPS and CUSTOM WORLD (endless land from a seed). Every custom world gets its own seed, which you can see or type. Worlds are saved the moment they're made and listed under CAREER with CONTINUE and DELETE. You pick your first truck by clicking its picture.
- **Every part fits every vehicle:** any engine, gearbox, suspension, wheel or tire works on any vehicle. Wheel wells trim and flare, the track widens, and long-travel arms lengthen to fit. A lifted Jesko on 40s now drives straight.
- **Engines forward:** the Blazer, Tacoma, 4Runner and Bronco engines sit further forward, as in the real trucks, with the weight moved too.
- **Shocks:** firmer rebound and less bump-stop kick. Bodies settle after drops instead of pogoing.
- **Terrain shadows:** hills and mountains cast shadows, and ridges shade the valleys at a low sun (shadows HIGH and up). Your car's shadow no longer goes through the hillside.
- **Lighting:** red brake lamps, warm reversing lights, lamp glow and beam haze, darker nights and stronger bloom.
- **Same world on every computer:** the Backcountry's rocks and objects are now in exactly the same places on Windows, Mac and Linux.
- **Recovery:** a reset, a tow home or a teleport sets the car down with a clear way out, never nose-in against a tree.
- **Keys:**
  - The parking brake is on P again; old settings files that had pause on P are fixed.
  - The pause key is gone: Esc's menu pauses single player, and multiplayer never pauses.
- **Dev free cam:** F8 with DEV KEYS on (or F12 > VIEW). Fly anywhere, through the ground if you like, then pick up, carry, turn and drop vehicles, spawn new ones at the crosshair, and switch into any of them.

Multiplayer protocol 10: host and guests all need 0.4.8.
