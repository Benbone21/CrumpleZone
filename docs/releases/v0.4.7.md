# Crumple Zone v0.4.7

- **One big map:** the Backcountry is now one 10 km world with the Open Country, Mountain and Frontier stitched on. Each region has its own country, look, trails and places. Picking another region of the map moves you there without a reload.
- **Backcountry trails:** about 43 km of rated trails (green, blue, black, faint two-tracks) with signs, markers and names on the M map. **COPPER PASS** (red) climbs past Copper Peak through the Notch, the Switchbacks, the Choke and a ledge along the Rimrock cliff. New places: Bald Knob fire lookout, four cabins, Slab City, Balanced Rock, the Tors, three obstacle trails and 11 new supply caches.
- **Procedural world (new map):** endless seeded land with mountains, valleys, rivers, deserts and a road and trail network. It slides with you as you drive, in multiplayer too.
- **Real terrain (new map):** a real place's elevation data as a drivable map.
- **Career worlds:** main menu > CAREER lists your worlds. A new world picks a name, map, difficulty and starting vehicle. Your trucks stay where and how you left them (damage, fuel, battery, paint). Swaps only at a station, into trucks you own. Autosaves every 3 minutes, with a backup. Old career progress carries over.
- **Smoother big map:** terrain streaming and autopilot planning moved off the frame (fixes 11-36 ms hitches every ~250 m and 330-460 ms freezes).
- **Fixed: Mac startup hang** with Apple's software renderer.
- **Fixed: G menu list racing** when the mouse sits at the top or bottom row.
- MAIN MENU returns to the new menu after an F5 reload or a --map launch.

Multiplayer protocol 9: host and guests all need 0.4.7.
