# Crumple Zone v0.4.14

- **Controller support:** play with a gamepad, and rebind every key and pad button in Settings.
- **Cars feel their weight:** tires flex and lose a little grip under heavy load, landings settle instead of bouncing, and dirt and gravel grip like real soil.
- **Simpler parts:** 3–4 plainly named parts per slot. Old saves move to the closest new part.
- **Endless procedural worlds:** no edge to reach, plus mesa country, a home biome per world, switchback trails and river banks that follow the water.

Multiplayer needs everyone on 0.4.14.
