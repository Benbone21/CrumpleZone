# Crumple Zone 0.4.0

Get into any vehicle on foot (your buddy's too), clearer prompts, a safer and easier level editor with every map back, people with real bodies and animation, and winch lines you walk out by hand.

## Downloads

- Windows: `CrumpleZone-0.4.0-windows-setup.exe`.
- Linux: `CrumpleZone-0.4.0-linux.deb` for amd64.
- Mac: `CrumpleZone-0.4.0-mac.dmg` (Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11+). It isn't notarized: the first time, right-click the app and choose **Open**.
- Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS`.

Multiplayer: players and servers must all run 0.4.0 (protocol 7). 0.3.0 can't join. Server owners: see `docs/server-update.md`.

## Changes

### On foot

- Get into any vehicle nobody is in: J near it, or E at the driver's door. Parked cars, wrecks, cars you spawned, and stopped AI traffic. Your old car stays parked with the handbrake on.
- Multiplayer: take over a buddy's empty truck (handed over through the host, dents and all) and hand it back; ride along when they're driving (C for the passenger seat, J to get out).
- Clearer prompts: markers on everything you can use nearby, the target outlined, "[HOLD E] REMOVE WHEEL" style prompts with the reason when it can't be done yet, and hints when you're near but not aiming. Aiming is more forgiving and doesn't flicker.
- Winch lines and tow straps are hooked up on foot: grab the line, walk it out, hook it to a tree, rock, stake or another truck. Z / Shift+Z still winch from the seat.
- People have real bodies: faces, clothes, gloves and boots, a walk and run with weight shift, idle breathing, jumps, and hands that hold your tools. In first person you see your own arms and boots. Nobody is drawn sitting in cars.

### Driving

- The camera holds the angle you set while you're stopped, and eases back behind the car once you drive.
- The cockpit view sits at the driver's eyes, and the steering wheel turns.
- Front wheels can no longer wind far past full lock and wreck the alignment (the 4Runner's "glitched tire").
- Speed-rating blowouts and the "TIRES OVER SPEED RATING" warning are off for now.

### Level editor

- Every map is back on the list and in the editor.
- Right-drag only turns an object you've selected; otherwise it moves the camera. M switches the gizmo between move and turn; O turns right-drag rotating off.
- Saves are crash-safe (written to a temp file, then swapped in), keep the previous version as a `.bak` (used automatically if a level won't load), autosave every 60 s, and F5 hot reload reopens the editor where you were.

### Fixes

- Mac and release builds fixed; the model sheet tool builds again.

## Known issues

- Physics are not bit-identical between Windows, Linux and Mac. Multiplayer between them works.
- Scanned rocks' collision can sit a little above the drawn rock on big scaled-up rocks (fix in progress).
