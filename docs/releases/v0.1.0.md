First packaged build of Crumple Zone, a node-and-beam soft-body driving sim for rock crawling, off-roading and crashes.

## Downloads
- **Windows (64-bit):** `CrumpleZone-0.1.0-windows-x64.zip`. Unzip and run `crumplezone.exe` (keep `SDL2.dll` and `assets/` beside it).
- **Linux (Debian/Ubuntu, amd64):** `crumplezone_0.1.0_amd64.deb`. Install with `sudo apt install ./crumplezone_0.1.0_amd64.deb`, then run `crumplezone` or pick Crumple Zone from the games menu. Built on Ubuntu 24.04, so it needs glibc 2.39 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04+, Debian 13+).

## What's in it
- **Vehicles:** Civic, Jesko, Bronco, 4Runner, rock buggy, turbo UTV and Tacoma, all soft-body, with real ladder frames on the trucks and steered solid axles on the buggy.
- **Maps:** proving ground, mountain, freeway, frontier and the new Backcountry, plus co-op multiplayer, a level editor and jobs.
- **Driving and recovery:** game-controller support (Xbox, PlayStation and generic pads), first person on foot, and a winch you can anchor almost anywhere (60 m reach).
- **Physics fixes:**
  - springs sized for the weight they really carry, so trucks sit at their design height
  - ABS and braking fixes, so the 4Runner brakes straight
  - parked cars wake when their controls move
  - live axles stay upright on hard landings
  - the buggy and UTV no longer break apart on spawn
- **Look:** photoscanned CC0 ground textures.

## Known issues
- Scanned props and trees aren't included yet: the source scans couldn't be downloaded for this build, so the scenery uses the built-in rocks and trees.
- Rock buggy and UTV tuning is still in progress:
  - the UTV is slow and twitchy in the whoops
  - the UTV's governor overshoots slightly (134 km/h)
  - the buggy's rear axle lands a few cm deeper than the trucks' on 2 m drops
- The 4Runner can bend its frame tail when it comes down off a tall ledge onto the lip, depending on speed.
- Not tested on real Windows hardware or a real gamepad: the Windows build was checked under Wine, and the controller code with virtual pads.
