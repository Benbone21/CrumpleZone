# Crumple Zone v0.4.15

- **Winch for every vehicle:** a big front-mounted winch you can fit in the garage. Vehicles that come with one can now take it off.
- **Steel bumper:** a heavy bumper and grille guard for any vehicle. The front crumples far less in a crash.
- **Repair markers:** each job has its own small marker on the part. Aim at one and hold E.
- **Every field repair can be done:** new tie rod, control arm, spring and knuckle repairs. A new career starts with a real toolbox and a spare tire.
- **Getting in:** aim at the door handle and press E. The get-out key only gets you out.
- **Front wheels hold up:** normal off-road hits no longer bend them at impossible angles, and field repairs put them straight. Damage you can't fix in the field takes a big crash.
- **Far respawns fixed:** respawning far from the start in a procedural world no longer breaks the ground, for you or the other players. You land at the nearest station.
- **Winch frame rate:** no more drop while carrying or aiming the winch line.

A car saved with damage loads undamaged once. Multiplayer needs everyone on 0.4.15.
