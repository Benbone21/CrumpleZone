# Crumple Zone 0.2.0

This release adds the Blazer and an overlanding lineup, hands-on field repair in a proper order, a much richer world, a full level editor, a debug screen and rebindable keys, and a hardened multiplayer server.

## Downloads

- Windows: `CrumpleZone-0.2.0-windows-setup.exe`.
- Linux: `CrumpleZone-0.2.0-linux.deb` for amd64.
- Mac builds will follow.
- Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS`.

Multiplayer: players and servers must run the same version. 0.2.0 cannot join games hosted by an earlier release, and the reverse.

## Changes

### Vehicles

- Add the Blazer (now the default vehicle), a garage for it, code-made models and the model lab. The overlanding lineup (vehicles 1 to 5) and the hidden Civic, UTV and buggy are in the garage.
- Part models are drawn on the cars. Blazer, Bronco, 4Runner and Tacoma get real ladder frames, and leaf packs hang from the hanger and shackle with their ends up, clamped on the axle tube.
- The 4Runner's front CVs hold as much traction as the Bronco's and Tacoma's, and a regression test covers its buggy-engine swaps in 4L.
- The Tacoma's bed is open at the back again and its tailgate closes flush.
- Keyboard throttle limit (5 to 100%), response INSTANT/SMOOTH/GENTLE, an optional pad cap, and a key that cycles 100/50/25/10.

### Field repair

- Repairs follow an order of steps: jack the corner, wheel off, unbolt, knock out, fit, torque, wheel on, lower. Each step names its tool, a prompt gives the next step, and a checklist shows the job. Progress is kept per corner.
- The jack lifts from a pad on the frame beside the wheel well. Animations show only the tool in your hand.
- Broken parts are shown snapped and glowing, bent ones kinked. Look at a part to see its name, condition and fix.
- E at the back opens an inventory menu with a lit icon for every tool, part and piece of gear. Everything in the back is drawn at a size you can see.
- Slings and anchors sit on the rock's real hull, and the winch aim ray stops at rocks and logs.

### World

- Section levels play on open country, with seven built-in section levels. A concrete spawn platform marks every start, and guests take their own place on it.
- Obstacles are built from scanned rock (laid, posed and sunk) on uneven ground; boxes are gone from the sections. Tyres no longer sink into the cliff, and there are more fallen rocks and ten big boulders on the cliff shelf.
- Biome music, water, and dunes (which no longer read past the map edge).
- Instanced trees with near, mid and far detail, detailed firs, oaks, snags, aspen, spruce and lodgepole, and logs with bark, moss and splintered ends. Trunk collision matches the drawn trunk. The tree mesh data shrinks from 181 MB to 4 MB.
- Lusher curved grass with biome kinds, flowers, wind and flattening under vehicles; a GRASS OFF/LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH setting.
- 13 plant kinds by biome (cactus, sagebrush, ferns, reeds, flowering shrubs, heather, tumbleweed and more), bush distance culling and denser undergrowth.
- Night sparkles on thin foliage are fixed.

### Level editor

- Brushes from one cell to 100 m with shapes, edges, splatter, noise and stripe patterns, roughen and terrace, and a drain brush. Q/E size the brush, Shift+wheel flies up and down.
- An object palette with icons and a ghost, exact picking, group select, Ctrl+drag box select, a transform gizmo with arrows and rings, and axis locks (Shift/Ctrl/Alt) while dragging. Right-drag turns objects. The grabbed point stays under the cursor.
- Stacking on rocks, snap to ground and a free object height that sticks. Blocks and logs ride the ground when it is sculpted.
- Map objects, props and section generator rocks are editable. PLANTS tab for plants.
- Playtest from here or from the start (F5/F6), F1 leaves a playtest, F2 opens the editor over your truck.
- Autosave, saving that never refuses, and DISCARD that returns to the last save.

### UI and controls

- F12 opens a dev menu; the grave key toggles DEV and GAMEPLAY keys.
- Every key action (57) is rebindable on a grouped keybinds page, hints show the bound key, and keybinds can be exported as defaults.
- F3 opens a debug screen (build, frame times, world, position, surface, vehicle and a per-wheel table, network); Shift+F3 shows a frame-time graph.
- One shared text field for every text box: Ctrl+V and Shift+Insert paste, Ctrl+C/X/A, cursor and selection, and key presses stay in the field.
- Controller rumble is now an event signal (hits, landings, breaks, rev limiter, rough-ground ticks) instead of a constant hum.
- The model viewer is crisp and no longer inherits the pause menu's blur.

### Multiplayer

- Host or join on any port: a PORT row in the host card, IP:PORT parsing, `CZ_PORT`, and clear errors.
- The dedicated server was reviewed and hardened (password, caps, timeouts, validated and fuzzed messages).
- The JOIN card takes a password (masked, with show and remember). A wrong or missing password asks again with RETRY.

### Performance and fixes

- Fix the freeze and crash on reloading the map by growing the scenery vertex buffer by doubling.
- Fix a heap corruption by locking the model mesh cache for worlds built on several threads.
- Memory use drops sharply with instanced trees and the smaller tree mesh.

## Known issues

Eleven physics tests are known to fail and were failing before this release: the frontier/current, top-speed, weight, tire-into-fender, rock-hits, whoops, shear and debead scenarios on specific vehicles. They are tracked and do not affect packaging.
