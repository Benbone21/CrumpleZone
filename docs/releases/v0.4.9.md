# Crumple Zone v0.4.9

- **New pause menu:** RESUME, MULTIPLAYER, SETTINGS, MAIN MENU, QUIT GAME.
- **Settings in tabs:** GRAPHICS, CONTROLS, AUDIO, GENERAL and DEV. Switch with Q/E, the mouse or the pad's shoulder buttons. F12 opens the DEV tab.
- **Autopilot and jobs on keys:** Shift+U picks an autopilot destination, and Shift+J opens jobs.
- **LEVELS on the main menu.**
- **Garage SWAP VEHICLE:** press G at a station to swap trucks. In career it lists the trucks you own.
- **Fixed:** MAIN MENU from inside a game always shows the new menu, never the old one.
- **Fixed:** in multiplayer each vehicle has one name tag, above its roof, instead of two covering the car.

Multiplayer: 0.4.8 and 0.4.9 can play together.
