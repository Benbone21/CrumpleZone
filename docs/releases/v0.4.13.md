# Crumple Zone v0.4.13

- **A bigger, more varied procedural world:** mountain ranges at a smaller scale, Backcountry-style rocks and knolls, trails that hug the land, Monument Valley–style mesas in desert country, cleaner edges between biomes, downed trees, huge rocks and wrecks.
- **Creeks and rivers run unbroken:** creeks are cut into the land, so they no longer stop and start, and lakes take over where rivers meet them.
- **Faster:** rocks are cheaper to draw, and a mountain area uses far less memory.
- **Career repairs cost money:** R opens a choice to pay for a repair on the spot, or less to respawn repaired at the nearest station. Sandbox is unchanged.
- **Multiplayer sessions fixed:** each time you host you get a new code, guests always load the host's current world, and when the host leaves everyone returns to the menu. Players spawn spread out instead of on top of each other.
- **No more ground grid:** ground textures are turned and blended per tile, so the repeat doesn't show.
- **Mac ground fixed:** on most maps the Mac refused to draw the ground at LOW quality and above.
- **Automatic updates now work:** 0.4.10 misread its own version, so it would have offered updates it didn't need. From this release on, updates arrive by themselves.

If you have 0.4.10 or older, install this release by hand once.
