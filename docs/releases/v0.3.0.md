# Crumple Zone 0.3.0

This release is much faster (about 3× the frame rate on a high-end GPU), adds tire grip by surface with tire temperature and blowouts, remodels the Bronco and Tacoma, rebuilds the proving ground, and brings multiplayer onto one shared clock.

## Downloads

- Windows: `CrumpleZone-0.3.0-windows-setup.exe`.
- Linux: `CrumpleZone-0.3.0-linux.deb` for amd64.
- Mac: `CrumpleZone-0.3.0-mac.dmg` (Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11+). It isn't notarized: the first time, right-click the app and choose **Open**.
- Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS`.

Multiplayer: players and servers must all run 0.3.0 (protocol 6). Older games are turned away with a message to update. Server owners: see `docs/server-update.md`.

## Changes

### Performance

- About 415–455 fps at 1440p HIGH on a Radeon 7900 XTX (0.2.0: about 130). Mac M1 LOW frames went from 61 to 15 ms.
- Fewer polygons and merged flat faces on cars and props, LODs for grass, rocks and cars, occlusion culling, compressed textures, a shader cache, background loading and smoother map switches.
- Graphics are picked for your hardware on first run, with a new POTATO preset; FPS targets including MATCH DISPLAY, 240 and 360.
- The game drops to 15 fps in the background.

### Driving and damage

- Tire grip depends on the tire and the surface: race slicks are the best on dry pavement and poor everywhere else, mud-terrains dig in mud, winter tires and chains grip on snow and ice.
- Tire temperature: cold tires grip less and warm up with use; winter areas start cold. Small tire marks by the gear show cold (blue), ready (green) or hot (red).
- No more speed governors. Each tire has a speed rating; push past it and it can blow out (the Jesko's stock tires are rated for its top speed). Field repair fits the spare.
- Fixed the 4Runner's (and others') front wheel locking far past full lock and wrecking the alignment.
- Fixed the Jesko's high-speed wobble.
- Crash damage is symmetric; the Blazer's nose is stronger, sides and rears dent more easily, panels count a little toward damage.
- The winch pays out properly.
- Tire tracks follow the tires and fade with distance and time.

### Vehicles

- The Bronco is remodeled after the 2021 4-door, with the doors meeting the roof.
- The Tacoma is remodeled as a TRD Pro double cab: grille, lamps, hood scoop, flares, rock rails, step bumper.
- The speedometer reads like a real one, calibrated to the factory tire; recalibrate with C in the garage.

### Proving ground

- A big grey grid pad for the test area and the model yard, with the natural world outside it.
- The model yard shows every design of every model, close together and labeled.
- Your saved level edits show in the section park.
- A lap timer on the loop road.

### Multiplayer

- One shared clock: every player sees each truck in the same place at the same moment, and the same time of day (the server owns it).
- Much less bandwidth: only what changed is sent, and far trucks update less often.
- Car-into-car crashes damage both cars.

### UI

- A contextual HUD that shows what matters right now (F9 / SHOW FULL HUD for everything); no more overlapping panels.

### Fixes

- The respawn point no longer moves after a hot reload.
- No crash when switching vehicles with key 5.
- Grass no longer disappears at the screen edge.
- Hangs and races in the worker threads and scenario runner fixed.

## Known issues

- Physics are not bit-identical between Windows, Linux and Mac. Multiplayer between them works.
