# Crumple Zone v0.4.16

- **Wrecks everywhere:** wrecked sedans, vans, SUVs, box trucks and flatbeds lie along the roads and trails, 44 of them in Backcountry. Search them on foot for parts and supplies.
- **Fuel cans:** carry a can, pour it into your tank, and top up the truck's spare can at a pump.
- **Gas pumps work:** take the pump hose and put it in your car to fill up. In career, station fuel isn't free any more.
- **Multiplayer distance limit:** in the procedural world, a warning tells you when you're getting too far from the others. Go too far and a countdown brings you back, so nobody ends up off the land.
- **Menus:** the host gets a LEVELS row on the multiplayer page, and Settings > GENERAL has an AUTOPILOT row.
- **Blazer rear end:** the solid rear axle no longer shakes.
- **Violent wrecks:** a truck that tumbles hard into rock no longer tears apart and falls through the ground.
- **Autopilot:** keeps clear of trees and poles instead of brushing past them.

Procedural worlds have changed, and multiplayer needs everyone on 0.4.16.
