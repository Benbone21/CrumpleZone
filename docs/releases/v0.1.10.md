# Crumple Zone 0.1.10

This release includes the performance and tire pressure work that arrived after 0.1.9, plus verification fixes.

## Downloads

- Windows: `CrumpleZone-0.1.10-windows-setup.exe`.
- Mac: `CrumpleZone-0.1.10-mac.dmg`, containing a universal Apple Silicon/Intel app and Applications shortcut. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized.
- Linux: `CrumpleZone-0.1.10-linux.deb` for amd64.
- The Mac ZIP is also available. Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS`.

## Changes

- Add saved adaptive quality controls in Settings and `--adaptive 0/1`. Adaptation targets 60 FPS by temporarily reducing render resolution and effects. Saved graphics choices remain intact, toggling off restores them, and recovery works under a 60 Hz VSync cap. Scripted captures remain fixed unless adaptation is explicitly requested.
- Hold 9 to deflate at 5 psi/s and 0 to inflate at 2 psi/s onboard or 8 psi/s at a station. Airing requires a stopped vehicle and stays within 12–40 psi.
- Lower pressure softens the sidewall and lowers the vehicle under load. The visible tire follows that loaded radius and widens slightly; rims retain their dimensions and the squash does not rotate with the tread. Remove an extra fixed-radius reduction that double-counted sag and broke the Bronco ledge test. Punctured/debeaded/missing tires keep their correct contact radius.
- Add tire-air hiss with smooth, callback-independent transitions and master mute. Bound the existing glass filter at low device sample rates.
- Retain the Windows thread-cache cleanup fix, soundscape improvements and Mac packaging from 0.1.9. Retry temporary macOS disk-image verification failures during packaging.

## Verification and limits

On this shared Linux host with AMD integrated graphics, two Backcountry Ultra runs measured approximately 50.0 ms/scene at fixed quality before and after these changes. Two adaptive runs averaged 38.2 ms/scene with render scale reduced to 50%: about 24% less render time, with reduced image quality. This test did not reach 60 FPS and does not establish a same-quality rendering speedup or performance on other GPUs.

The new adaptive-quality and pressure checks pass. All 45 audio checks, five importer tests and 12 offscreen rendering/menu checks pass. Serial/four-worker simulation fingerprints agree for separated vehicles and pileups. Native Windows/Mac checks and packaged-application checks gate publication. Hosted Mac graphics use Apple Software Renderer and do not measure real Mac GPU performance.

Existing physics failures remain documented in the [verification report](https://github.com/Benbone21/CrumpleZone/blob/v0.1.10/docs/release-verify-0.1.10.md). No baseline or assertion ranges were loosened. Engine voices still use procedural fallback: no real-car engine recording banks are bundled. The evaluated field-recording download and generated audition WAVs are not shipped as engine assets.
