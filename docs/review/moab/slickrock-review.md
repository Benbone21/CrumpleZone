# Moab slickrock: before and after (branch wip/moab-slickrock)

Each pair is the same camera (`--eye X,Z,H,YAW,PITCH`, 10:00, `--photo --adaptive 0`, 1600x900, offscreen), "before" built
from wip/moab-trails (9e420ec) with its own map files, "after" from this branch. References are photos viewed on the web, not copied.

| Pair | Camera | Compared against | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 Hell's Revenge domes, wide | 4900,300,45,-42,-6 | NAIP 2024 0.6 m over the fins (data/real/moab/source/hires/naip2024, block e627600 n4270800); traildamage.com Hell's Revenge photo [derek_up_a_steep_hill](https://traildamage.com/wp-content/uploads/trails/hells_revenge/031024/derek_up_a_steep_hill.jpg) | Better, not there. The fins on the skyline now have the lidar's real shape and read as banded rock, and the foreground is bare pinkish rock with orange sand in the hollows instead of an orange dune field with pale blotches. Still wrong: the near ground is too flat and smooth (it is the 10 m DEM outside the lidar box; the real Sand Flats are knobbly domes), far too little brush, the trees are green cones, and sand patches have hard painted edges. |

Overall: the "stupid looking rocks" (grey and green granite rows on orange humps) are gone everywhere, and the ground reads as
sandstone, not sand dunes. It does not yet look like real Moab: the ledges are small and regular where the real ones are big,
rounded and stacked, and outside the lidar the land is still the smooth 10 m DEM.
