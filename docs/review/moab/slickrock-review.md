The after shots were retaken after the three missing FlamingGorge 1 m tiles were fetched: every named obstacle is now on lidar,
and on lidar an obstacle's walls, fins, pitch and tilt are the survey's (only the ledges' faces are authored slabs).

Overall: the "stupid looking rocks" (grey and green granite rows on orange humps) are gone everywhere, and the ground reads as
sandstone, not sand dunes. It does not yet look like real Moab: the ledges are small and regular where the real ones are big,
rounded and stacked, and outside the lidar the land is still the smooth 10 m DEM.
