# Hell Boy: research notes for the vehicle

Hell Boy (since 2025 also "HB") is the off-road recovery wrecker built by "Wrecker Rick" Murphy of Murphy's Diesel / Murphy's Towing in northern Arizona. It is a Ram 5500 crew cab on 58-inch tires with planetary axles and a boom wrecker body. The game's `hellboy` vehicle models the **2025–2026 state** of the truck. Earlier states (2022 wheel lift and 20-inch tires, the 2025 SEMA wrap) are listed for reference only.

Confidence labels used below:

- **stated**: said by Rick on camera, or printed in a feature article
- **seen**: visible in frames in `docs/reference-images/hellboy/`
- **inferred**: my reasoning from what is seen or stated

## History

- **Feb 2022.** Rick bought a used 4WD Ram 5500 wrecker in Dallas with more than 270,000 miles on it. *stated*
  - It had a 12-ton wrecker body, two 12k Ramsey winches and a wheel lift.
  - He renamed it from "Bloody Mary" to Hellboy, "red like Hellboy", and built a front stinger bumper shaped like Hellboy's horns. *stated*
- **Jul–Oct 2023, full rebuild** after repeated transfer-case failures. *stated* [ORX]
  - Added: AxleTech planetary axles with front and rear steer, a custom four-link, Big Shocks, 58-inch tires and compound turbos.
  - The wheel lift came off ("at least 1,500" lb lighter). Since then he tows with slings and anchors with fold-down spades. *stated*
- **Mar 2024.** Nitrous added. The truck was weighed hanging from the 75-ton rotator. *stated + seen*
- **2025.** Red paint, white Murphy's Diesel door logo, black boom lettered MURPHYS DIESEL. *seen*
  - At SEMA it showed in a red and black Warrior Winches wrap. *seen*
- **2026.** Tube doors with Murphy's Diesel panels inside a full exo-cage. EGT fire at Matt's Off-Road Games. *seen + stated*

## What the game models, and what it doesn't

**Modelled:**

- Ladder frame under a crew-cab body with a sculpted Ram-style shell.
- Red paint over black trim and black wheels.
- 58-inch tires on beadlocks at low pressure.
- Steered front solid axle on a four-link with coilovers.
- Rear solid axle on a four-link; the air bags become an equivalent spring rate.
- Air lockers front and rear.
- Two-speed transfer case.
- Allison-style six-speed automatic with a torque converter.
- Compound-turbo 6.7 Cummins torque curve at about 700 hp and 2,700 N·m.
- Final drive 6.86.
- 6.7 t total mass.
- Front winch, using the game's winch-and-strap recovery.

**Drawn, not simulated.** These are visual extras. Their mass is carried as mass items so the weight and balance are right.

- Boom, toolboxes, headache rack and cage
- Horn stinger bumper and rear bumper
- Light bar and beacon

**Not modelled yet:**

- **Four-wheel steer.** The game steers only the front axle.
- **A working boom.** Neither lifting nor towing on the boom works.
- **Hydraulic winches and air-shift details.**
- **Nitrous.**

Four-wheel steer and a working boom are each a new subsystem. Per the task brief, neither was built without the user's go-ahead.
