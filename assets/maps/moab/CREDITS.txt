Moab, Utah (the REAL: MOAB map)

Heights (dem.tif, backdrop.tif): USGS 3D Elevation Program, 1/3 and 1 arc-second DEMs, tile n39w110. Public domain.

Trails, roads, the Colorado River's line and the named places and obstacles (osm.txt, and the way and node ids in moab.txt):
(c) OpenStreetMap contributors. Available under the Open Database License 1.0, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
osm.txt is a derived database made by tools/osm_extract.py from one Overpass query of 38.4957-38.6443 N, 109.680-109.490 W
(data as of 2026-11-09); it is shared under the same licence.

Obstacle sizes, trail ratings and course text: written for the game from the research in docs/plans/moab-trails.md
(Red Rock 4-Wheelers ratings, BLM, Grand County, Funtreks and onX descriptions); no source gives obstacle dimensions.
